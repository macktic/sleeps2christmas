# Greek Bible data

`greek_bible.sqlite` is generated by `import_greek_bible.php` from the locally
downloaded LXX and SBLGNT source files. The importer validates 27,827 LXX verse
records, 7,939 SBLGNT verse records, and 4,433 exceptional English-to-LXX
versification mappings before replacing the database.

## LXX

The LXX source is the public-domain ancient Greek Septuagint distributed by
eBible.org for Orthodox Media Network. Retain `LXX/grclxx_about.htm` with the
project because the distributor requests that its about text accompany public
sharing of the work.

## SBLGNT

## Versification

English-to-LXX reference conversion uses the English-to-LXX subset of the
STEP Bible TVTMS data, distributed under CC BY 4.0.

Data provided by STEP Bible (www.STEPBible.org) under CC BY 4.0. Original work
by Tyndale House, Cambridge.
